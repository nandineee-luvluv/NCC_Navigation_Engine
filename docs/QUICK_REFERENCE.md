## Fire Resistance Levels (FRL)

FRL is expressed as **X/Y/Z** where:
- **X** = Structural adequacy (stability) in minutes
- **Y** = Integrity (smoke & flame penetration) in minutes
- **Z** = Insulation (temperature rise) in minutes

### Common FRL Requirements

| Building Class | Typical FRL | Example |
|----------------|------------|---------|
| Class 1-2 Residential | 60/60/60 | Standard residential buildings |
| Class 5 Commercial | 90/90/90 | Office buildings |
| Class 6 Industrial | 60/60/60 | Factories |
| Class 9-10 High-rise | 120/120/120 | Tall buildings |

## Limit States

### Ultimate Limit State (ULS)
- Focuses on **strength and stability**
- Considers maximum loads and minimum resistance
- Ensures structure doesn't collapse
- Factor of safety typically 1.5-2.0

### Serviceability Limit State (SLS)
- Focuses on **deflection and cracking**
- Considers normal service conditions
- Ensures comfort and functionality
- Limits deflection to span/250 or similar

## Common Design Actions

### Permanent Loads (G)
- Self-weight of structure
- Permanent finishes
- Fixed equipment
- Calculated from material density × volume

### Imposed Loads (Q)
- Occupancy loads (people, furniture)
- Varies by building use
- Typical values: 1.5-5.0 kPa for residential, 2.5-5.0 kPa for commercial

### Wind Loads (W)
- Dynamic pressure from wind
- Depends on location, terrain, building height
- Calculated using AS 1170.2
- Varies significantly by region

### Earthquake Loads (E)
- Inertial forces from ground motion
- Depends on importance level and location
- Calculated using AS 1170.3 or 1170.4
- Higher for critical facilities (IL 3-4)

## Material Specifications

### Concrete (AS 3600)
- **Strength Grade:** f'c (e.g., 25, 32, 40 MPa)
- **Exposure Class:** A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, C2, C3, C4, C5-M, C5-H
- **Slump:** 50-200 mm depending on application
- **Reinforcement:** AS 3600 or AS 4671

### Steel (AS 4100)
- **Grade:** 250, 300, 350, 450 MPa
- **Section Types:** I-beams, channels, angles, tubes
- **Bolts:** Grade 8.8, 10.9 (AS 1111, AS 1112)
- **Welds:** AS 1554 (structural steel welding)

### Timber (AS 1684)
- **Species:** Hardwood, softwood
- **Grade:** F5, F8, F11, F14, F17, F22, F27, F34
- **Moisture Content:** 12-15% for structural use
- **Preservative Treatment:** H3, H4, H5 as required
